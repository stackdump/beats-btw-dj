# Scopes — beats scope visualizations in the native app

Branch `phase5-scopes`. Ports the scope / stage visualizations of beats.bitwrap.io
(`public/lib/ui/scope.js`, `scope-model.js`, `stage.js` at beats-bitwrap-io `095d030`) into
dj-vevr-se, per deck and on the master, fed by the engine's own audio taps and live net
state. Not merged, not pushed: for review.
